## 3. Market Gap
There is a lack of transparent pricing and specific millet meal offerings in the market. Customers may be looking for a dedicated millet meal service with clear pricing and a variety of options.

## 4. Recommended Positioning
> "Healthy millet meal boxes delivered fresh, Rs 150 per meal."

Why this price: Mummy's Cloud Kitchen starts at Rs 180 per meal, while Zero Cals and Millet Express do not list prices. Setting the price at Rs 150 positions the offering competitively below Mummy's Cloud Kitchen, appealing to price-sensitive health-conscious consumers.

## 5. 30-Day Launch Plan
- Week 1: Create a simple website and social media profiles to establish an online presence.
- Week 2: Partner with local gyms and health clubs to promote the meal boxes to health-conscious individuals.
- Week 3: Conduct a pricing test by offering a discount for the first 50 customers to gather feedback and adjust pricing if necessary.
