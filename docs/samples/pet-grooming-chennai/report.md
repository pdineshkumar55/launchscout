## 3. Market Gap
There is a lack of clear, upfront pricing for grooming services in Chennai, which busy pet owners prefer. Customers want straightforward pricing without hidden fees, as seen in complaints about competitors' lack of transparency.

## 4. Recommended Positioning
> "At-home pet grooming with clear pricing starting at Rs 899 per session."

Why this price: The recommended price of Rs 899 is positioned below ThePetNest and PawSpace, both priced at Rs 999. This competitive pricing strategy aims to attract busy pet owners looking for transparent and affordable grooming services.

## 5. 30-Day Launch Plan
- Week 1: Create a simple website and social media profiles to establish an online presence.
- Week 2: Partner with local pet supply stores for cross-promotions and discounts.
- Week 3: Test pricing by offering introductory rates at Rs 899 for the first month.
- Week 4: Set a target to acquire 50 customers through social media ads and local flyers.
