## 2. What Customers Complain About

**What customers praise:**

- Affordable prices for handpicked sarees (ST Silks) [5]
- High-quality products and friendly service (Om Saravana Silks and Readymades) [6]
- Good service for regular customers (Om Saravana Silks and Readymades) [6]
- Unique collections and good service (Shubhankari Silks) [4]
- Nice collection with a good shopping experience (Shubhankari Silks) [4]

## 3. Market Gap
There is a market gap for high-quality silk sarees with transparent pricing and superior finishing, as evidenced by customer complaints regarding the stitching quality at Shubhankari Silks.

## 4. Recommended Positioning
> "Premium silk sarees with exquisite finishing starting at Rs 3,800 each."

Why this price: The recommended price of Rs 3,800 is positioned below ST Silks' Rs 4,200, offering a competitive advantage while ensuring high-quality finishing that addresses customer concerns. This price point also aims to attract customers looking for quality without the premium price tag.

## 5. 30-Day Launch Plan
- Week 1: Establish a social media presence on Instagram and Facebook to showcase saree collections and engage with potential customers.
- Week 2: Partner with local wedding planners and event organizers to promote sarees for upcoming weddings and festivals.
- Week 3: Conduct a pricing test by offering an introductory discount on the first few purchases to attract initial customers.
- Week 4: Set a target to sell at least 50 sarees by the end of the month through online and local channels.
